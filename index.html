SECURITY, WITHOUT THE MYSTERY

Know what protects your vault.

01 / Secret-key encryption

AES-256-GCM encrypts your complete vault and authenticates its contents. Every save uses a fresh 96-bit random nonce.

02 / Password-based keys

PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA-256 uses 600,000 iterations and a random 128-bit salt to derive your encryption key. Your master password is never saved.

03 / Randomness & hashing

Passwords use crypto.getRandomValues with rejection sampling. Password strength is a heuristic; it is not a breach check.

04 / Network & web security

No accounts, analytics, or external resources. Vault data stays in this browser. A deployed version must use HTTPS and protect against script injection.

05 / Public keys, PKI & key exchange

This single-device design needs no public-key exchange. A future sync service would need TLS, certificate validation, and an independently designed end-to-end protocol.

06 / Limits & post-quantum security

AES-256 provides a strong symmetric security margin. This educational app is not independently audited. Malware, browser extensions, or injected scripts can access an unlocked vault.

The vault locks after 5 minutes of inactivity. Keep encrypted backups: losing your master password or browser data can permanently lose your credentials.
